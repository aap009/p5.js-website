  <p>Draws a circle.</p>

  <p>A circle is a round shape defined by the <code>x</code>, <code>y</code>, and <code>d</code>
  parameters.

  <code>x</code> and <code>y</code> set the location of its center. <code>d</code> sets its width
  and height (diameter).

  Every point on the circle's edge is the same distance, <code>0.5 * d</code>, from its center.

  <code>0.5 * d</code> (half the diameter) is the circle's radius.

  See <a href="/reference/p5/ellipseMode/">ellipseMode()</a> for other ways to set its position.</p>
