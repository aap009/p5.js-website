  <p>Copies a region of pixels from one image to another.</p>

  <p>The first parameter, <code>srcImage</code>, is the

  <a href="/reference/p5/p5.Image">p5.Image</a> object to blend.</p>

  <p>The next four parameters, <code>sx</code>, <code>sy</code>, <code>sw</code>, and
  <code>sh</code> determine the region

  to blend from the source image. <code>(sx, sy)</code> is the top-left corner of the

  region. <code>sw</code> and <code>sh</code> are the regions width and height.</p>

  <p>The next four parameters, <code>dx</code>, <code>dy</code>, <code>dw</code>, and
  <code>dh</code> determine the region

  of the canvas to blend into. <code>(dx, dy)</code> is the top-left corner of the

  region. <code>dw</code> and <code>dh</code> are the regions width and height.</p>

  <p>The tenth parameter, <code>blendMode</code>, sets the effect used to blend the images'

  colors. The options are <code>BLEND</code>, <code>DARKEST</code>, <code>LIGHTEST</code>,
  <code>DIFFERENCE</code>,

  <code>MULTIPLY</code>, <code>EXCLUSION</code>, <code>SCREEN</code>, <code>REPLACE</code>,
  <code>OVERLAY</code>, <code>HARD_LIGHT</code>,

  <code>SOFT_LIGHT</code>, <code>DODGE</code>, <code>BURN</code>, <code>ADD</code>, or
  <code>NORMAL</code></p>
