description: |
  <p>Calculates coordinates along a Bézier curve using interpolation.</p>
  <p><code>bezierPoint()</code> calculates coordinates along a Bézier curve using the
  anchor and control points. It expects points in the same order as the
  <a href="/reference/p5/bezier/">bezier()</a> function. <code>bezierPoint()</code> works one axis
  at a time. Passing the anchor and control points' x-coordinates will
  calculate the x-coordinate of a point on the curve. Passing the anchor and
  control points' y-coordinates will calculate the y-coordinate of a point on
  the curve.</p>
  <p>The first parameter, <code>a</code>, is the coordinate of the first anchor point.</p>
  <p>The second and third parameters, <code>b</code> and <code>c</code>, are the coordinates of the
  control points.</p>
  <p>The fourth parameter, <code>d</code>, is the coordinate of the last anchor point.</p>
  <p>The fifth parameter, <code>t</code>, is the amount to interpolate along the curve. 0
  is the first anchor point, 1 is the second anchor point, and 0.5 is halfway
  between them.</p>
